Emma co-convened a discussion meeting at the Royal Society, London, UK on 'Marine Biodiversity loss, Fishing and Climate Change'. The two-day event saw invited speakers from across the globe present their latest research in marine biology, with a strong focus on plankton. 

Emma chaired the session on fishery impacts to biodiversity, Cordelia and Sean presented posters, and Holly and Genesis got some excellent experience networking. We are excited for to see where the connections made take us!
